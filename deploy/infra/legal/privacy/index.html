1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide, information collected automatically, and information obtained from third parties.

1.1 Information You Provide

This may include account and profile information, payment and transaction details, feedback, support requests, and contact details such as account name, mobile number, or email address.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

This may include device information, usage data, location information, cookies, and similar technologies.

1.3 Information Obtained from Third Parties

We may receive information from social media services, advertising partners, single sign-on platforms, related entities, business partners, other customers, or your authorized representatives.

1.4 User-Generated Content

Where the Service lets you create, upload, publish, store, or share text, images, videos, comments, or bullet comments, we process that content and associated metadata to provide the relevant interactive features. Public content may be visible to unspecified users, so do not disclose sensitive personal information in it.

You are responsible for ensuring that content you publish is lawful, appropriately authorized, and does not infringe the rights of others. We may review, remove, restrict, or report content that violates law, this Policy, or the applicable user agreement. Deleted content may remain in copies, caches, or material already shared by others.

2. How We Use Information

2.1 Providing Services

We use information to create and maintain accounts, provide browsing, search, video-on-demand, social and support features, process orders through relevant payment providers, maintain security, and operate the Service.

Usage logs may include account, search, network, language, access time, page-view, refresh, follow, subscription, favorite, and sharing activity. Device data may include device and operating-system type, app version, resolution, package and device settings, advertising identifiers, and IP address.

2.2 Improving Services

We may use information to develop and improve the Service, personalize content and recommendations, respond to requests, diagnose faults, measure service health, and deliver technical, security, support, and administrative notices.

2.3 Marketing and Advertising

You may opt out of marketing through the unsubscribe mechanism in the communication or by contacting support@zikodrama-tv.com. Service-related messages may still be sent when necessary. Device or service settings may provide additional controls for advertising and tracking technologies.

2.4 Security and Compliance

We use information to protect the Service, prevent fraud, hacking, cheating, spam, and other unlawful or unauthorized activity, investigate incidents, and enforce our terms. Security tools may process operating-system, CPU architecture, application package, and signature information locally to support hardening and anti-tampering measures.

2.5 Notifications and Permissions

With your consent and subject to device settings, we may send push notifications. Specific features may request storage or device permissions. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not share personal information outside ZikoDrama without consent except when needed to provide or improve the Service, work with contracted providers, comply with law, protect rights and safety, resolve disputes, complete a corporate transaction, or respond to a lawful request.

Recipients are required to process information for lawful, necessary, specific purposes and apply suitable confidentiality and security measures. De-identified information that cannot reasonably identify a person may be processed and shared as permitted by law.

4. Storage and Protection of Information

Information may be stored in the United States or other countries where we, our affiliates, partners, or service providers operate. We use reasonable technical, organizational, access-control, monitoring, and incident-response measures. No Internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of, or a portable copy of your information. We may need to verify your identity and may decline a request where a legal exception applies. ZikoDrama does not currently sell personal information for monetary consideration.

6. Processing of Minors’ Information

ZikoDrama is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information without legally required consent. If you believe a child’s information has been collected, contact support@zikodrama-tv.com so it can be removed as required by law. Users under 18 should use the Service with a parent or legal guardian’s consent.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred internationally in accordance with applicable law. We take appropriate steps to protect it, although the law in the destination may provide a different level of protection from your country of residence.

8. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy or as required or permitted by law, taking account of its amount, nature, sensitivity, risks, and the purposes of processing.

9. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal information and associated account data by emailing support@zikodrama-tv.com. We will process the request within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law.

10. Updates to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The latest version will be posted with its effective date, and we recommend reviewing it periodically.
